Last updated: November 6, 2026

01 Scope & publisher

Coin Thief is published by RSG INTERACTIVE. In this policy, “RSG INTERACTIVE,” “we,” “us,” and “our” mean RSG INTERACTIVE, operated by Rohit Shekhawat. This policy applies to Coin Thief and to this website when they link to it.

This policy does not replace privacy notices provided by Epic Games, a platform operator, or another service that independently processes information. Those providers act under their own privacy policies and terms.

02 Information we may collect

Information you provide: If you email us for support, privacy requests, or business enquiries, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, message, and attachments. We do not collect information through a web form on this website.

Technical information: Visiting a website can make technical information available to the website host or service providers, such as an IP address, browser and device type, operating system, pages requested, timestamps, and security logs. We do not use this website to request a user account, payment card, precise location, contacts, or other sensitive information.

Game information: The information collected by Coin Thief depends on the platform and features enabled in the released build. Potential categories include a platform identifier, display name, game progress, session information, device information, crash or diagnostic data, and purchase or entitlement information. These are examples, not a claim that every category is collected. The release-specific implementation must be confirmed before launch.

03 How information is used

We may use information you provide to respond to you, provide support, investigate reports, communicate about Coin Thief, protect the game and website, comply with legal obligations, and establish or defend legal claims.

Where a game service is enabled, that service may use information needed to authenticate players, provide the requested feature, maintain sessions, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, or improve reliability. We do not claim to use personal information for targeted advertising. Any future advertising or similar tracking must be reflected in an updated policy.

04 Multiplayer and online services

Coin Thief may include online or multiplayer features depending on the released build. If enabled, those features may require a platform account and may process identifiers, display names, matchmaking or lobby details, connection information, gameplay events, and moderation or anti-abuse information. Other players may see information that you make visible through the platform or the game, such as a display name or game-session activity.

Epic Online Services (EOS): No EOS integration is present in the website repository reviewed for this policy, and RSG INTERACTIVE cannot confirm from this repository whether the Coin Thief build uses EOS. EOS is therefore not claimed as an active service by this policy. If the released build uses EOS, Epic may process the data required by the specific EOS products selected, under Epic’s Privacy Policy. The actual EOS products, data categories, retention, and player controls must be confirmed and added before release.

If the released build is single-player and has no online features, this section does not apply to that build.

05 Third-party services

Website fonts: This website loads fonts from Google Fonts using fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. A browser request may disclose technical request information, such as an IP address and user-agent information, to Google. Google’s privacy policy governs its processing.

Platform and game providers: Epic Games and the platform through which you obtain Coin Thief may process account, device, purchase, gameplay, and technical information independently. Their privacy notices and settings apply to their processing.

At the time of this update, this website repository contains no confirmed game configuration for analytics, advertising, crash reporting, payment, account management, voice chat, anti-cheat, or social-media SDKs. Before release, RSG INTERACTIVE must identify every SDK or provider actually included in the Coin Thief build. This policy must then name each provider, explain its purpose and data categories, link to its privacy information, and describe retention and available controls.

06 Information sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate Coin Thief or respond to you, with platform or game-service providers when you use their features, when you direct us to do so, or when disclosure is required to comply with law, protect safety, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect our rights.

Epic Games, a platform operator, or another third-party provider may process information independently under its own privacy policy. Information controlled by that provider may need to be accessed, corrected, or deleted through that provider.

07 Security, retention & deletion

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information and service involved. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain information only for as long as needed for the purpose collected, to provide support, resolve disputes, maintain security, keep required business records, or comply with law. Email correspondence is normally retained only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes. Game and platform retention periods are controlled by the relevant implementation and provider and must be confirmed before release.

To request deletion of information that RSG INTERACTIVE controls, email rohitsingh3dart@gmail.com with the subject “Coin Thief Privacy Request.” Explain what information or account the request concerns. We may verify the request and may retain information where required by law or needed for security, disputes, or legitimate records. Deletion from Epic Games or another provider must be requested from that provider where it controls the information.

08 Children’s privacy

Coin Thief and this website are not intended to request personal information directly from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child through this website. The game’s audience, age rating, parental controls, consent process, and service-specific collection practices must be confirmed before release, especially if the game is directed to children or is likely to be used by children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

09 Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; and to complain to a data protection authority.

To make a request, email rohitsingh3dart@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity and may have lawful reasons to limit or decline a request. Platform-account requests should also be made to the relevant platform or service provider.

10 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Coin Thief, this website, its services, or applicable law changes. We will publish the revised policy on this page and update the “Last updated” date. The revised policy applies from publication unless a different effective date is stated.
